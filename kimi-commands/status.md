Call `attacca_status`, `get_handoff`, `get_identity_handoff`, and `task_list`
through the Attacca MCP
server. Summarize the workspace, current actor and role, context version,
lead, shared project handoff, exact AI identity handoff, open or claimed tasks,
pending decisions, and anything requiring user
attention. If the checkout is unattached or stale, invoke the native setup flow
yourself rather than handing the human a command or selecting a workspace
silently. $ARGUMENTS
